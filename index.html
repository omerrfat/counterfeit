How to inspect

Every day the museum hangs eight exhibits on one theme. Three are forgeries: plausible claims that are false. Your torch is the only light, and the battery is draining.

  • Move the torch over an exhibit to read it. Click or tap it to select.
  • Stamp it Genuine or Forgery, and choose how sure you are.
  • A right call earns its multiplier. A wrong one costs it. Leaving a stamp off costs nothing but earns nothing.
  • Rule on all eight, then close the case to turn the lights on.

Keys: Tab moves between exhibits, G or F stamps, 1 2 3 sets confidence.
